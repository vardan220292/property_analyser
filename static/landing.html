One tool, three audiences

Buyer's agents present. Investors analyse. Clients explore the numbers on their own terms.

For buyer's agents

Save a scenario, add a client, share a branded link. The proposal snapshots what you shared; keep editing freely afterwards. See every client action — viewed, commented, approved — in one place.

For investors

Acquisition costs and duty, LMI, IO/P&I loans, negative gearing, depreciation, land tax, borrowing capacity, exit and CGT — across a whole portfolio, in every Australian state.

For clients

The differentiation

The industry sells conviction. We sell verification. When a client can move the interest rate themselves and watch the annual shortfall change, the conversation stops being a pitch and becomes a decision. That is the product.
